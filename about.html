✦ MILESTONES SINCE 2018

Our journey

2018

Incorporation and Humble Beginnings

Cloud Production Ltd. was formally incorporated in the year 2018, with a small dedicated team at its inception.

2019

Market Entry and Offshore Expansion in Japan

CPL initiated its market entry into Japan, concurrently introducing offshore services as part of our strategic expansion efforts.

2020

Strategic Partnership with Arousal Tech

Established a significant partnership with Arousal Tech, marking them as a valuable collaborator in our endeavors.

2021

Product Diversification and Service Expansion

Cloud Production Ltd. (CPL) embarked on the development of in-house products, including an HRMS and a job portal, with the intention of offering these solutions to external clients as part of our extended service portfolio.

2022

Local & Global Expansion

We initiated the provision of services both locally and globally, with a particular emphasis on Japan as a priority market.

2023

Launched a Flagship Office in Dhaka

Cloud Production Ltd. (CPL) relocated to a new physical office location, marking a significant step in our journey toward fostering innovation, attracting fresh talent, and cultivating new business ideas.
